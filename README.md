## Hi, I'm Lennart

I build production AI systems and explain them to the people who have to decide on them.

Trained as a biologist (M.Sc., Aarhus University) and a science journalist (DMJX), I spent two decades writing for Berlingske, Ingeniøren, Illustreret Videnskab, Ugeskrift for Læger and others before moving into AI full-time. Making specialist work land with non-specialists turned out to be the skill that matters most in AI too.

### What I work on

- **[docujAI](https://docujai.com)** — strategic AI advisory, courses and board workshops for small and medium-sized businesses. Practical, production-grade AI, and saying plainly when AI is not the answer.
- **Folkets Avis** — Lead AI Engineer. A multi-agent editorial system in daily production: source-grounded article generation, internal RAG, and open-source monitoring with every claim traceable to its source.
- **Humanice** — a human-in-the-loop LLM content platform for editorial teams.

### Climate communication

I co-founded **[klimaleksikon.dk](https://klimaleksikon.dk)** in 2008 — Denmark's most visited learning resource on climate change — and its English sibling **[climate-encyclopedia.com](https://climate-encyclopedia.com)**.
